Toys for the children

Children come with their families for an evening of performances, a cupcake and a drink, and each one leaves with a new toy. In 2022 the church handed out another 500 small toys while caroling the night before.

food baskets in 2022

Groceries for their families

Volunteers take food baskets to families' homes in the days before Christmas, so the holiday table is covered as well as the gifts.

A Christmas tradition since 2010

Led by Pastor Ian Zaldivar, the outreach has grown with the island, and local businesses have joined in.

  1. 2010

    Living Word Church starts its Christmas toy drive, with the aim of bringing joy to all of San Pedro's children.

  2. 2018

    1,100 toys handed out at the Hon. Louise Sylvestre Sporting Field, each with a cupcake and a drink, plus food baskets for families.

    Reported by Ambergris Today
  3. 2022

    1,200 toys at the giveaway, plus 500 small toys and 150 food baskets on the caroling night before. Caye Bank, its clients and partners back the outreach with a major donation.

    Reported by The San Pedro Sun
  4. 2024

    The Toy + Grocery Outreach: groceries delivered door to door, then a toy giveaway with children's performances at the San Pedro High School auditorium on December 23.

    Reported by The San Pedro Sun
  5. 2025

    Another year of toys for the island's children, pictured at the top of this page.

  6. 2026

    This year's outreach. Your donation buys this December's toys and groceries.
